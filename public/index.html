Three ways to work together

Pick the level of involvement your company needs now. Many engagements start small and change shape as the business does.

Fractional CIO

A senior technology leader on a monthly retainer. Roadmap, budget, vendors, security posture, and a seat at the leadership table, without a full-time executive hire.

Best for: growing companies with an IT team but no CIO.

Interim CIO

Full-time leadership for a defined stretch: a departure, a turnaround, or a sale process. Stabilize the team, steady operations, and hand off cleanly to the next leader.

Best for: a vacant CIO seat or a company in transition.

Transformation & integration

Project leadership for work that can't wait: ERP and HR platform implementations, cloud migrations, security uplift, and technology diligence and integration for acquisitions.

Best for: a deal, a deadline, or a platform change.
